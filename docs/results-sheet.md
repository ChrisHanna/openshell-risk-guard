# Results sheet

Risk Guard on AKS with OpenShell, Kata sandboxes, and SPIFFE workload identity. Every number links to the report that produced it. "Scripted"
means the traffic was generated by the author's own agents; none of it is an independently held-out evaluation.

## Security

| Result | Value | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Routine reads refused | 0 of 195 (95 % interval 0–1.9 %) | [detection results](detection-results.md); scripted, 4 h window |
| First-time dangerous calls refused | 6 of 6 (interval 61–100 %) | same; small sample |
| Repeat dangerous calls refused | 20 of 20 | same; 16 were refused because a restriction carried over |
| Direct-path bypass tests from a real sandbox | 18 of 18 expected results, plus 1 documented gap | [egress bypass](../conformance/egress-bypass-after-fence.json); the gap: a compromised supervisor can reach in-cluster HTTP directly |
| Failure containment | 7 of 7 live cases; 6 more tied to named tests | [report](../conformance/failure-containment.json); no destructive request reached the destination |
| New connections after a quarantine | refused at once | [report](../conformance/quarantine-tunnel-bounded.json) |
| Open connections after a quarantine | end within 120 s (measured 81 s and 112 s) | same; not instant, and not claimed to be |
| Decision replay on live data | 4 agents, 468 decisions, all reproduced | `replay-verify` on the running deployment |

## Cost and size

About **$350 a month** in compute for the whole demonstration cluster (four small VMs) at list price; one node (about $70) hosts Risk
Guard and its proxies. See [sizing and cost](sizing-and-cost.md).

## What is not here

Backup and restore are not yet proven, there is no high availability, and a clean first-time install by a second person has not been done. The full list, in priority order,
is [production gaps](production-gaps.md).
